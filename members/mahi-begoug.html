Mahi Begoug

Mahi Begoug is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Data-driven Analysis of Software (DAS) Lab, Concordia University, Montréal, Canada, under the supervision of Prof. Emad Shihab.

He received his Ph.D. in Software Engineering from École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS), Université du Québec, Canada, in 2026, under the supervision of Prof. Ali Ouni. Prior to his Ph.D., he earned a Research Master's degree in Software Engineering from the University of Montpellier, France, in 2022, and an Engineering degree in Computer Science from the École nationale supérieure d'informatique (ESI), Algiers, Algeria, in 2021.

His research interests lie at the intersection of Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and Software Quality, with a particular emphasis on developing intelligent approaches to improve software development, maintenance, and evolution.

During his Ph.D., his research focused on Infrastructure as Code (IaC), investigating software quality, defect prediction, automated repair, technical debt, and the evolution of infrastructure configurations. His work combined empirical software engineering, machine learning, and automated program analysis to understand and improve the reliability and maintainability of IaC systems.

Currently, his research focuses on AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE), particularly LLM-based coding agents and multi-agent systems. His ongoing research investigates the reliability, effectiveness, and efficiency of autonomous coding agents, including agent collaboration and coordination, persistent memory, coding-agent trajectories, and self-improving agentic systems. He is particularly interested in understanding how these systems make decisions, interact with software repositories, and can be evaluated and improved throughout the software engineering lifecycle.
